## 3. Discount Authority

All discounts are calculated from list price on the subscription portion of the deal. Professional services may not be discounted by more than 10% without Finance approval.

| Discount off List | Approver | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10% | Account Executive | No approval needed; document reason in CRM |
| 11–20% | Director of Sales | Approve in CRM within 1 business day |
| 21–30% | Director of Sales and Director of Finance | Requires deal desk review |
| 31–40% | CEO | Requires written business case |
| Above 40% | Not permitted | Exceptions only for strategic partnerships approved by the CEO and Board |

## 4. Standard Concessions

- **Multi-year:** Up to an additional 5% discount for a 3-year commitment, within the approver's authority.
- **Competitive displacement:** When replacing FakeCo, Account Executives may request up to 20% off list with Director of Sales approval.
- **Non-profit and education:** 25% discount, pre-approved.
- **Price increases at renewal:** Standard uplift of 7%. Caps below 5% require Director of Finance approval.

## 5. Payment Terms

- Standard terms: Net 30.
- Net 60 requires Director of Finance approval.
- Monthly billing is not offered for any tier.
