This FAQ answers common questions about Vantage Technologies benefits for the 2026 plan year. It will be finalized before open enrollment begins.

## Open Enrollment

**When is open enrollment?**
Open enrollment for the 2026 plan year runs from October 15 through November 5, 2025. Elections take effect January 1, 2026.

**What can I change during open enrollment?**
You can enroll in, change, or waive medical, dental, and vision coverage; add or remove dependents; and set your Health Savings Account (HSA) and Flexible Spending Account (FSA) contributions. Outside of open enrollment, changes are allowed only after a qualifying life event such as marriage, birth, or adoption.

**Where do I enroll?**
Log in to the benefits portal through Okta. Benefits information sessions will be held on October 16 and October 22; recordings will be posted on the intranet.

## Medical, Dental, and Vision

Vantage offers two medical plans: a PPO plan and a high-deductible health plan (HDHP) with an HSA. Vantage pays 90% of employee premiums and 75% of dependent premiums for both plans. For employees who choose the HDHP, Vantage contributes $1,000 per year to the HSA for individual coverage and $2,000 for family coverage. ## 401(k) Retirement Plan

**Does Vantage offer a 401(k) match?**
Yes. Vantage matches 100% of the first 4% of eligible pay you contribute, plus 50% of the next 2%, for a maximum match of 5%.

**When does the match vest?**
Company matching contributions vest immediately.

**When can I enroll?**
You can enroll or change your contribution rate at any time through the retirement plan provider. New hires are automatically enrolled at 3% after 30 days unless they opt out.

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

**What is the PTO policy?**
Full-time employees accrue 15 days of PTO per year in their first two years, 20 days in years three through five, and 25 days after six years. Up to 5 unused days carry over to the next year. Employees also receive 8 paid sick days and 11 company holidays, including one floating holiday. See the Parental Leave and PTO Policy for full details.

**Is PTO changing in 2026?**
No changes to PTO accrual are planned for 2026.

## Other Benefits

- **Life and disability insurance:** Basic life insurance at 2x base salary and long-term disability coverage at no cost to employees.
- **Employee Assistance Program (EAP):** Free, confidential counseling sessions for employees and household members.
- **Wellness stipend:** $50 per month for gym memberships, fitness classes, or wellness apps.
- **Learning budget:** $1,500 per year for courses, certifications, and conferences with manager approval.
