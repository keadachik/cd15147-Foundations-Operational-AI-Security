## 1. Purpose

This summary shows the aggregate distribution of performance ratings after mid-year calibration. It supports planning for the April merit cycle and October promotion review. Individual ratings are stored in the HR system only and are not included here.

## 5. Observations

- Sales has the widest spread, with the highest share of both top ratings and ratings of 2 or below. This matches quota attainment results for the first half.
- Engineering is within guidelines. The share of "Exceeds" ratings rose 3 points from last year, driven by the playbooks launch.
- Finance and HR recorded no ratings of 1.
- 49 employees (11.9%) received a rating of 2 or 1. Managers have been asked to complete performance improvement plans or documented coaching plans by August 31.
