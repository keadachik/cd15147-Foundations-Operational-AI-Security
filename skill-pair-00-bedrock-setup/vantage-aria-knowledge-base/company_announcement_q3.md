# Q3 All-Hands Recap

**From:** Office of the CEO
**Date:** October 3, 2025
**Classification: Internal**

Thank you to everyone who joined the Q3 all-hands, in person in Austin or online. Here is a recap of what we covered.

## Business Highlights

- **Customer growth:** We added 38 new customers in Q3, including our largest Enterprise logo to date.
- **Retention:** Gross revenue retention held steady at 93%, and net revenue retention reached 112%.
- **Product:** Automated playbooks shipped to all Professional and Enterprise customers in August. Early adoption is strong, with 61% of eligible accounts creating at least one playbook.
- **Aria:** Our internal AI assistant, Aria, is now available to all employees in Slack and the intranet. Aria answers questions about company policies, IT procedures, and product information. Please share feedback in #aria-feedback.

## Office Expansion: Denver

Following strong growth in our Finance and Sales teams, we signed a lease for a larger Denver office. The new space in the LoDo neighborhood has room for 120 people and will open in **January 2026**. Robert Patterson and Marcus Webb are leading the move planning. Denver-based employees will receive details about parking, badge access, and move dates in November.
