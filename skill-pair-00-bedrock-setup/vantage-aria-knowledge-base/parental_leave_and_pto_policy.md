# Parental Leave and PTO Policy

| Field | Value |

## 1. Paid Time Off (PTO)

### Accrual

Full-time employees accrue PTO each pay period based on years of service:

| Years of Service | Annual PTO | Accrual per Semi-Monthly Pay Period |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 years | 15 days | 5.0 hours |
| 3–5 years | 20 days | 6.67 hours |
| 6+ years | 25 days | 8.33 hours |

Part-time employees accrue PTO on a prorated basis.

### Carryover

Employees may carry over up to **5 unused days** into the next calendar year. Any balance above 5 days is forfeited on January 1. Unused PTO is paid out at separation where required by state law.

### Requesting PTO

- Submit requests in the HR system at least two weeks in advance for absences of three or more days.
- Managers should respond within three business days.
- Update your Slack status and calendar while you are out.

## 2. Sick Time

Employees receive **8 paid sick days** per year, separate from PTO. Sick time may be used for your own illness or medical appointments or to care for a family member. Sick time does not carry over.

## 4. Parental Leave

### Eligibility

All full-time employees who have completed 90 days of employment are eligible for parental leave upon the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child. Eligibility is the same for all parents regardless of gender.

### Benefit

- **16 weeks of fully paid parental leave**, to be taken within 12 months of the birth, adoption, or placement.
- Leave may be taken continuously or in up to two blocks of at least four weeks each.
- Birthing parents may also be eligible for short-term disability benefits, which run concurrently with parental leave.

### Pay and Benefits During Leave

Your compensation continues at your regular base pay rate throughout paid parental leave. Health, dental, and vision coverage continue on the same terms, and PTO continues to accrue.

### Returning to Work

Employees may request a phased return, working 60% of their normal schedule at full pay for up to four weeks after leave ends. Coordinate a phased return with your manager and People Operations.

### How to Request

1. Notify your manager and People Operations at least 30 days before your planned leave start date, when possible.
2. Submit the Leave Request form in the HR system.
3. People Operations will confirm your leave dates and coordinate with the benefits provider.

## 5. Other Leave

- **Bereavement leave:** Up to 5 paid days for the loss of an immediate family member; 2 days for extended family.
- **Jury duty:** Paid for the duration of service.
- **Volunteer time:** 1 paid day per year.
