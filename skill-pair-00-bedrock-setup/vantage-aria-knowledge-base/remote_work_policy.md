Vantage is a remote friendly company. This policy defines expectations for employees working fully or partially remote.

# Remote Work Policy

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Policy owner | People Operations |
| Effective date | March 1, 2024 |
| Classification | Internal |

## 1. Work Arrangements

Vantage supports three work arrangements:

- **Office-based:** Works from a Vantage office (Austin or Denver) four or more days per week.
- **Hybrid:** Works from a Vantage office one to three days per week.
- **Fully remote:** Works from an approved home location in a country where Vantage has a legal entity.

Your arrangement is recorded in the HR system and agreed with your manager.

## 2. Eligibility

- Most roles are eligible for hybrid or fully remote work.
- Employees must have completed their first 30 days, including onboarding, before switching to fully remote, unless hired into a remote role.
- Roles that require on-site hardware access (for example, some IT and facilities positions) may be limited to office-based or hybrid arrangements.
- Relocating to a different state or country requires advance approval from People Operations because of tax and payroll implications.

## 3. Core Hours

All employees are expected to be available for collaboration between **10:00 a.m. and 3:00 p.m. U.S. Central Time**, Monday through Friday. Outside core hours, employees may set a schedule that works for them and their team. Keep your Slack status and calendar up to date so colleagues know when you are available.

## 4. Communication Expectations

- Respond to Slack messages from your team within four business hours during core hours.
- Use video for team meetings and one-on-ones when practical.
- Document decisions in shared channels or project tools rather than in private messages.

## 5. Equipment

- IT issues every employee a company laptop, a monitor, and a headset.
- Remote and hybrid employees may use the home-office stipend described in the Expense Reimbursement Policy for additional equipment.
- Company equipment remains Vantage property and must be returned when employment ends.
- Report lost or stolen equipment to the IT Service Desk at x4400 within 24 hours.

## 6. Security Expectations

Remote work must meet the same security standards as office work.

- Connect to the Vantage VPN before accessing internal systems.
- **Do not use public Wi-Fi** (coffee shops, airports, hotels) to access or process customer data, even with the VPN. Use a trusted home network or a mobile hotspot instead.
- Lock your screen whenever you step away from your device.
- Do not let family members or others use your company laptop.
- Do not print Confidential or Restricted documents at home.
- Keep CrowdStrike Falcon and operating system updates running as required by the Information Security Policy.

## 7. Workspace

Employees should work from a quiet, safe space that allows for confidential conversations. Vantage does not reimburse home internet service, but employees with connectivity problems can request a mobile hotspot from IT.

## 8. Travel to Offices

Fully remote employees may be asked to travel to an office up to four times per year for team planning events. These trips are reimbursed under the Expense Reimbursement Policy.

## 9. Changes to Arrangement

Either the employee or manager may request a change to a work arrangement with 30 days' notice. People Operations reviews all requests to confirm eligibility.

## 10. Questions

Contact People Operations at peopleops@vantagetech.example or x4430.
