# Expense Reimbursement Policy v2.0

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Policy owner | Finance / Accounts Payable |
| Effective date | July 1, 2023 |
| Status | Current |
| Classification | Internal |

This policy replaces v1.0 (January 2023).

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how Vantage Technologies ("Vantage") employees are reimbursed for reasonable, necessary business expenses incurred while performing their job duties.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees of Vantage. Contractors are reimbursed according to the terms of their individual agreements.

## 3. General Principles

- Expenses must be directly related to Vantage business.
- Employees should choose the most cost-effective option that meets the business need.
- Personal expenses, including upgrades for personal convenience, are not reimbursable.
- Managers are responsible for reviewing and approving expenses submitted by their direct reports.

## 4. Reimbursable Categories and Limits

| Category | Limit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Meals (while traveling) | $75 per day | Includes tips. Alcohol is not reimbursable. |
| Client meals | $125 per attendee | Requires names of attendees and business purpose. |
| Lodging | $225 per night | Higher limits in high-cost cities with manager approval. |
| Airfare | Economy class | Book at least 14 days in advance when possible. |
| Ground transportation | Actual cost | Rideshare, taxi, or rental car (compact or mid-size). |
| Personal vehicle mileage | IRS standard rate | Commuting miles are excluded. |
| Conferences and training | Actual cost | Requires manager pre-approval. |
| Software and subscriptions | $50 per month | Must not duplicate company-provided tools. |
| Home-office stipend | $500 per year | New in v2.0. See Section 5. |

## 5. Home-Office Stipend

Employees classified as remote or hybrid under the Remote Work Policy are eligible for a home-office stipend of up to **$500 per calendar year**. Eligible purchases include desk chairs, desks, monitors, lighting, and headsets. The stipend does not roll over and is prorated for employees who start after January 1. Items purchased with the stipend belong to the employee.

## 6. Receipts

Itemized receipts are required for any individual expense of **$20 or more**. Credit card statements alone are not accepted as receipts. Lost receipts must be documented with a Missing Receipt Declaration signed by the employee's manager.

## 7. Submission Deadline

Expense reports must be submitted within **45 days** of the date the expense was incurred. Reports submitted after 45 days require written approval from the Controller.

## 8. Approval and Payment

1. Employee submits the expense report in the expense system with receipts attached.
2. Manager reviews and approves or rejects within 5 business days.
3. Accounts Payable audits the report and schedules payment.
4. Approved reimbursements are paid by direct deposit within 7 business days of Accounts Payable approval.

## 9. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

- Alcohol
- Personal entertainment (movies, minibar, spa services)
- Traffic or parking fines
- Travel for family members or companions
- Home office items above the annual stipend amount
