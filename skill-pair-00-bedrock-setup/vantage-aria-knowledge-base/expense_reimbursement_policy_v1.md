## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how Vantage Technologies ("Vantage") employees are reimbursed for reasonable, necessary business expenses incurred while performing their job duties.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees of Vantage. Contractors are reimbursed according to the terms of their individual agreements.

## 3. General Principles

- Expenses must be directly related to Vantage business.
- Employees should choose the most cost-effective option that meets the business need.
- Personal expenses, including upgrades for personal convenience, are not reimbursable.
- Managers are responsible for reviewing and approving expenses submitted by their direct reports.

## 4. Reimbursable Categories and Limits

| Category | Limit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Meals (while traveling) | $50 per day | Includes tips. Alcohol is not reimbursable. |
| Client meals | $100 per attendee | Requires names of attendees and business purpose. |
| Lodging | $200 per night | Higher limits in high-cost cities with manager approval. |
| Airfare | Economy class | Book at least 14 days in advance when possible. |
| Ground transportation | Actual cost | Rideshare, taxi, or rental car (compact or mid-size). |
| Personal vehicle mileage | IRS standard rate | Commuting miles are excluded. |
| Conferences and training | Actual cost | Requires manager pre-approval. |
| Software and subscriptions | $50 per month | Must not duplicate company-provided tools. |

## 5. Receipts

Itemized receipts are required for any individual expense of **$25 or more**. Credit card statements alone are not accepted as receipts. Lost receipts must be documented with a Missing Receipt Declaration signed by the employee's manager.

## 6. Submission Deadline

Expense reports must be submitted within **30 days** of the date the expense was incurred. Reports submitted after 30 days may be denied at Finance's discretion.

## 7. Approval and Payment

1. Employee submits the expense report in the expense system with receipts attached.
2. Manager reviews and approves or rejects within 5 business days.
3. Accounts Payable audits the report and schedules payment.
4. Approved reimbursements are paid by direct deposit within 10 business days of Accounts Payable approval.

## 8. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

- Alcohol
- Personal entertainment (movies, minibar, spa services)
- Traffic or parking fines
- Travel for family members or companions
- Home office furniture or equipment not issued by IT
