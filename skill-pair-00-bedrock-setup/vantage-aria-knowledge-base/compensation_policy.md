## 1. Compensation Philosophy

Vantage Technologies aims to pay competitively and fairly for the work people do. Our approach is based on three principles:

- **Market-based:** Salary bands target the 60th percentile of the B2B software market for companies of similar size, using third-party survey data refreshed each year.
- **Consistent:** Employees in the same level and role family are paid within the same band, regardless of work location within the United States.
- **Performance-linked:** Movement within a band is driven by sustained performance and growth in scope.

## 3. Placement in Band

- New hires are typically placed between the band minimum and the band midpoint.
- Offers above the midpoint require approval from the department head and HR.
- Offers above the band maximum are not permitted. Exceptional candidates should be evaluated for the next level.

## 4. Merit Cycle

- Vantage runs one merit cycle per year, effective **April 1**.
- Merit budgets are set by Finance and typically range from 3% to 5% of eligible payroll.
- Increases are based on the prior year performance rating and position in band.
- Employees hired after October 1 of the previous year are not eligible for the current cycle.

## 5. Promotions

Promotions may occur during the merit cycle or at a mid-year review on **October 1**. A promotion increase is typically 8% to 12%, or enough to reach the new band minimum, whichever is greater.

## 6. Equity

- Employees at L4 and above receive a new-hire equity grant.
- **Equity refresh:** Eligible employees are considered for an annual refresh grant during the merit cycle. Refresh grants vest over four years with no cliff.
- Grant sizes are set by level and performance rating and are approved by the Board Compensation Committee.

## 7. Pay Transparency

Salary band ranges are included in all U.S. job postings. Employees may request their own band from their manager or HR at any time. Individual salary information is confidential and must not be shared by managers or HR staff outside of approved processes.
