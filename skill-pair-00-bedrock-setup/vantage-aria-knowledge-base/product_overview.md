# Vantage Platform Overview

**Classification: Internal**

## What Vantage Does

The Vantage platform helps mid-market and enterprise B2B companies manage the full customer lifecycle in one place: account onboarding, usage analytics, renewals, and customer health scoring. Customers use Vantage to reduce churn, spot expansion opportunities, and give account teams a single view of every customer.

## Product Tiers

Vantage offers three product tiers: Starter, Professional, and Enterprise.

### Starter

Designed for small customer success teams getting started with structured account management.

- Up to 25 users
- Account and contact management
- Basic customer health scores (usage and support ticket signals)
- Standard dashboards
- Email and Slack notifications
- Integrations: Salesforce, HubSpot, Zendesk
- Email support with next-business-day response

### Professional

For growing teams that need automation and deeper analytics.

- Everything in Starter
- Unlimited users
- Configurable health score models
- Automated playbooks (onboarding, renewal, and risk workflows)
- Product usage analytics with event-level data
- Custom dashboards and scheduled reports
- Additional integrations: Snowflake, Segment, Jira, Intercom
- Okta and Azure AD single sign-on
- Chat and email support with 8-hour response

### Enterprise

For large organizations with complex data, security, and compliance needs.

- Everything in Professional
- Multiple business units and workspaces
- Predictive churn and expansion models
- Aria for Customers, an AI assistant that summarizes account activity
- Custom data retention and regional data residency (U.S. or EU)
- Audit logs and SIEM export
- Role-based access control with custom roles
- Sandbox environment
- Dedicated customer success manager and 24/7 support with 1-hour response for critical issues
- 99.9% uptime SLA

## Security and Compliance

All tiers include encryption in transit and at rest, and the platform is SOC 2 Type II audited annually. Enterprise customers can request a copy of the latest SOC 2 report and penetration test summary under NDA.

## Competitive Positioning

Our main competitor in the mid-market is FakeCo. Compared with FakeCo, Vantage differentiates on:

- Faster time-to-value (typical onboarding in 4 weeks versus 10+ weeks)
- Native product usage analytics instead of a third-party add-on
- Configurable health scores available starting at the Professional tier
